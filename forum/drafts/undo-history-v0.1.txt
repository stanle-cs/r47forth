[b]undo-history v0.1[/b]

This package gives R47 a multi-level undo and a screen to look at it. Stock UNDO remembers one step. With the package it keeps walking: press UNDO again for two operations back, again for three, a couple dozen deep for ordinary stack work.

[list]
[*]UNDO steps back one level per press (the first press is the stock undo, the rest come from the package).
[*]REDO steps forward again, until you run something new (a new operation drops the redo trail, same as any editor).
[*]U.HIST opens the history view. UP and DOWN move the selection, ENTER restores the selected level, EXIT leaves.
[*]HCLR empties the history (the stock one-step undo stays loaded).
[*]All four sit on the STK menu and in the catalog, and they can be assigned to keys.
[*]The UHIST system flag routes f-shifted UP to the view (normal mode only, BST everywhere else is untouched). Off by default, set it with SF in SYSFL.
[/list]

I keep the flag set and go f-UP straight into the view.

[attachment=2]undo-attach-1-history-view.png[/attachment]

That shot is the view after a short session with some arithmetic, a transposed 2x3 matrix, SLVQ on 1 -3 2, a complex number, and one press of UNDO.

Each row is one level, newest on top. Columns are level number, what ran next, and what X held at that moment. Operations show their real names. A dash means a plain value entry. (now) is where you were when you started undoing. The * marks where undo currently stands and the inverted row is your selection.

A level holds the whole stack plus LASTx, the flags, and the statistical sums when there are any. Restore puts all of it back.

[attachment=1]undo-attach-3-restored.png[/attachment]

That's ENTER on the SLVQ row from the first shot. The matrix is back in T and the three coefficients sit in Z, Y and X as they were before the solver ran.

[b]The ~ mark[/b]

[attachment=0]undo-attach-2-gap.png[/attachment]

Every level stores a full copy of the stack, and the whole history lives in 4 KB (the pool only has about 5 KB to spare before upstream's own matrix tests start failing). A state over about 1 KB, a big matrix or a long string, doesn't fit and I skip it. The next stored level carries a ~ to say something is missing between it and the row below. The numbering stays consecutive and the mark is the only trace. Undo still works across the gap. One press there covers more than one operation, and you can't land inside it.

[b]Limits[/b]

[list]
[*]History is session-only. RESET and loading a saved state clear it.
[*]Depth depends on the values. Plain reals give about 25 levels at stack size 4, about 16 with SSIZE8, 48 at most.
[*]Solver and integrator internals stay out of the history (SLV alone would flood it with iteration states). One solver call is one level.
[*]A running program doesn't capture (the stock undo skips those too).
[*]Undo restores system flags along with the stack. Stock UNDO already did that.
[/list]

[b]Install[/b]

Unzip the attachment into packages/undo-history and build with the package active:

[code]make sim CUSTOM_PKG=packages/undo-history[/code]

It composes with forth-core:

[code]make sim CUSTOM_PKG=packages/forth-core,packages/undo-history[/code]

For the calculator the target is make dmcp5r47 with the same CUSTOM_PKG. It adds 3.9 KB of flash, and the history takes its 4 KB from the memory pool at reset (you'll see it in the FLGS free-memory line).

Package base is r47forth commit faf9d698c. Upstream's full test suite runs green with the package active, alone and combined with forth-core. My own tests add about thirty cases for the undo behavior and the view. Everything above is from the simulator. I haven't run this build on a real DM42n yet, so make a calculator backup and flash at your own risk. The zip has COPYING inside. The licence is GPL-3.0-only, inherited from c43.
